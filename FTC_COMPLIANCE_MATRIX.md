# ⚖ Federal Trade Commission (FTC) Non-Compliance Matrix

This document acts as a legal regulatory ledger tracking how the promotional and marketing structures deployed by Algoriphi Inc. (OmniPhi) directly violate active Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines and consumer protection laws.

The FTC maintains an absolute mandate: **"There is no AI exemption from the laws on the books."** Deceptive claims utilizing AI hype carry civil penalties.

---

## 🚨 Flagged Violations & Regulatory Frameworks

### 1. Deceptive Performance Claims & "AI-Washing"
* **Target Law:** **FTC Act Section 5 (Prohibiting Unfair or Deceptive Practices)** & *Operation AI Comply* (Enforcement Sweep).
* **The Violation:** OmniPhi’s public marketing claims its system can read real-time macro-news feeds (like social media posts) to make instant, profitable market executions. 
* **The Reality:** Marketing a third-party LLM middleware wrapper as an instantaneous news-trading environment without rigid, scientifically documented substantiation constitutes severe "AI-washing" under current FTC enforcement parameters.

### 2. Fabricated Consumer Sentiment & Astroturfing
* **Target Law:** **FTC Final Rule on Deceptive Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 255)**.
* **The Violation:** Organizing coordinated 5-star Trustpilot review profiles to manipulate public perception before a retail customer baseline even exists.
* **The Reality:** Forensic footprints show that a cornerstone 5-star review anchoring their early profile was explicitly posted by **Nik Vassev**—the company's own paid PR director—without a clear, conspicuous disclosure of his material corporate connection.

### 3. Undisclosed Commercial Affiliate Funnels
* **Target Law:** **FTC AI Endorsement "Double Disclosure" Mandate**.
* **The Violation:** Utilizing a network of affiliate content creators (such as @getintoai on instagram) to deploy automated "Comment-to-DM" onboarding links.
* **The Reality:** These funnels fail the mandatory standard requiring prominent, on-screen text within the first 5 seconds disclosing both the paid affiliate relationship and the automated/synthetic nature of the message delivery pipeline.
